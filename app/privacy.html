Mail Cleaner

Privacy

Last updated October 12, 2026.

In short

Mail Cleaner runs in your browser, or with the desktop edition on your own computer. It has no server of its own, no accounts, no analytics and no ads. Your mail goes straight between your browser and Microsoft, Google or your mail server. Nobody else receives it, including the author.

What it reads and changes

  • After you sign in, it lists your folders and reads, for each mail in the folders you scan, the sender, subject, date, size, read state, whether it has attachments and its unsubscribe headers, to show you who sends you what.
  • It reads a mail's content only when you open that mail in the app.
  • It moves, archives, marks or deletes mail, creates block rules or filters and sends unsubscribe requests only after you confirm it in the app.
  • With Gmail it reads your Google storage quota through the Google Drive API, with a permission that can't see your files.

Where it keeps things

  • What it reads from your mailbox stays in the page's memory and is gone when you close or reload the tab.
  • Sign-in tokens stay in the tab's session storage and are gone when you close it. Signing out removes them, and for Google also withdraws the app's access. In the desktop edition you sign in in your own browser, and the app keeps the sign-in in memory until you close it.
  • Your settings and your remove list are kept in your browser's local storage, on your device.
  • Desktop edition with IMAP: your mail password is only sent to your mail server, over an encrypted connection. On Windows it's stored on your computer, encrypted with your Windows account; on macOS and Linux the app keeps it only until you close it.
  • Files you export from the desktop edition go to your Downloads folder.

Who it talks to

Only Microsoft (sign-in and Microsoft Graph) or Google (sign-in, the Gmail API and the Google Drive API); the unsubscribe addresses senders put in their mails, when you choose to unsubscribe; and in the desktop edition your IMAP server, plus autoconfig.thunderbird.net, which receives only the domain of your address when the app looks up your server. The text for an AI is something you copy or download yourself: the app never sends it anywhere.

Google user data

Mail Cleaner's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only for the features you see in the app. It is not stored on any server, not transferred to anyone, not used for advertising and not read by people.

Your control

Sign out at any time. You can also remove the app's access in your Microsoft account (Privacy, Apps and services) or at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
